Fearless 4.3.0 removes networks that have shut down or can no longer be reached, including Moonbeam, Moonriver and Polygon zkEVM. Polkadot and Kusama staking moved to Asset Hub and is not available in this version; existing stakes are safe there. Your keys are not affected, and funds on a removed network stay under your recovery phrase. The app now also switches away from unreachable nodes automatically and includes security fixes.
