Fearless 4.3.1 brings Polkadot and Kusama staking back on Asset Hub, where your stake moved: see and manage your DOT and KSM stake, nominations, unbonding, rewards and nomination pools again. Networks that shut down or can no longer be reached are removed; your keys are not affected, and funds on a removed network stay under your recovery phrase. The app also switches away from unreachable nodes automatically and includes security fixes.
