Fearless 4.2.0 brings public TON features, Coinbase provider integration, migration from WalletConnect to Reown SDK, and a new price service with remote asset sync. Includes platform/toolchain upgrades required by Google Play, relaychain staking accuracy fixes, hardened TON dapp URL validation/history pagination, stability and UX improvements, CI/lint/signing hardening, refreshed translations, and Polkadot SDK stable2503 compatibility via config alignment. Staged rollout is in progress — please report any issues via Settings → Support.
