favorites name, and marks it unavailable, so no key or chain account is
deleted. The bundled `local_chains.json` inventory and the frozen
portable-wallet receiving policy derived from it are unchanged.

### Staking, crowdloans and history

- Polkadot, Kusama and Westend no longer offer relay staking, nomination pools
  or crowdloans. Since the Asset Hub migration their relays hold no staking
  ledger (`Staking.CurrentEra` is empty, with 0 nominators and 0 pools).
  Staking lives on the Asset Hubs, which this version does not support yet.
  A saved choice that is no longer offered shows "Select Network", and the
  chooser opens with nothing preselected. The saved choice is never replaced
  on its own.
- The unreachable crowdloan contribution screens and their partner
  integrations are deleted. Legacy crowdloan recovery on the DOT and KSM
  balance screens is unchanged.
- History and staking indexers that can no longer answer are removed, so
  those networks show history as unsupported. The
  `squid.tachi.soramitsu.co.jp` zone is a lame delegation; SubQuery's hosted
  projects and the Subsquid gs-main squids answer 404.

### Fixes found during release review

Two independent review rounds (Codex gpt-6-astra) and a pass over a separate
Codex line found these. Each is covered by tests that fail without the fix.

- WalletConnect: the dApp-signing pause is checked again before and after the
  key is read, before a broadcast (once the node is reached) and before a
  signature is returned.
- Sends: a send is bound to the reviewed account and to its asset's chain
  before anything is signed. A history write that fails after the network
  accepted a transfer no longer reports the send as failed, so the confirm
  screen cannot send it twice.
- Ethereum: EIP-1559 transfers tip the node's priority fee and cap the price at
  the base fee plus that tip; before, they paid the whole cap. The fee shown is
  estimated for the amount being sent; the amount-free estimate is shown only
  while the node refuses the entered amount.
- Backups: a Google backup is encrypted with the password the user confirmed;
  the password fields are locked while it is saved and after. It backs up the wallet and
  derivation paths the user reviewed. Confirming the phrase of an added
  account no longer marks the whole wallet backed up.
- Staking payouts are computed in exact integer arithmetic, as the runtime
  does. An era with no reward points no longer fails the whole list.
- Guava 33.7.2 fixes CVE-2026-102554. `gradle/verification-metadata.sha256`
  now matches the metadata that #1266 changed.

### Accepted risks

- `net.i2p.crypto:eddsa:0.3.0` has signature malleability (CVE-2020-36843),
  and no fixed release exists. The app signs with it, and verifies only its
  own signatures and portable-wallet proofs. A malleated signature still
  proves the key. Replacing the library touches key signing, so it is left for
  a later release.
- The build-script classpath carries Netty, commons-compress, BouncyCastle
  1.79, jose4j, jdom2 and Kotlin 2.1.10 advisories. They affect build tooling
  only, not the shipped app.

  15 of 66 files, so it is planned as 4.3.1.
