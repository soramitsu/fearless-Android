## Contents

4.3.1 is release 4.3.0 (`release/android-4.3.0`, PR #1270) plus Polkadot and
Kusama staking on their Asset Hubs. Since the Asset Hub migration the relays
hold no staking state, so 4.3.0 users could not see or manage DOT and KSM
stake. The work comes from the Codex line
`codex/android-validator-search-repair-20261029`, ported commit by commit onto
4.3.0 rather than merged:

- staking: `6deb6042a`, `0ad163322`, `d124e6d0c`, `8725d646e`, `9b484a808`,
  `2c9ccf8a7`, `d8c4bfe86`, `b9eb70177`, `61fe33b13`, `7255f256a`,
  `e33080ea9` (without `fa0371c75` and the transitional pin `7f6acc7e0`);
- validator search: `e8a2619ed`, `19e8553a2`, `72aab0049`, `29c264df2`;
- fearless-utils `fc9719e`, `05d20f2` and the signing tests through
  `8cbd704`, into the app-owned `runtime-utils`.

### Asset Hub staking

- Polkadot Asset Hub (DOT) and Kusama Asset Hub (KSM) offer staking and
  nomination pools for their native asset. The relays never offer staking,
  pools or crowdloans again.
- Calls follow the current Hub runtime (Polkadot and Kusama Asset Hub
  2005001): `bond` without a controller, `nominate`, `bond_extra`, `unbond`,
  `rebond`, `withdraw_unbonded`, `set_payee`, `chill`,
  `payout_stakers_by_page`, and the pool calls. Tests sign each one through
  the app's builder over the live metadata and compare the payload with
  independent polkadot-js vectors.
- Pending payouts are found on chain from `ErasStakersPaged`,
  `ErasStakersOverview` and `ClaimedRewards` at one finalized block. Every era
  of the history depth is searched, including eras of validators the account
  no longer nominates or that removed it (`kick`); only pages that
  `ClaimedRewards` does not list are read. A page is offered only while its
  validator is still bonded: the runtime refuses a payout for a validator
  without a ledger (`NotStash`), so a validator that withdrew everything is not
  offered, and cannot make a payout fail or stop the claims after it.
- The era clock comes from the relay (`Babe`, `Session`) and `BondedEras`; the
  relay stays connected in the background while Hub staking is on.
- The unbonding period is read per account: nominators the runtime does not
  slash unbond in `NominatorFastUnbondDuration` eras. When the account's period
  cannot be read, the full `BondingDuration` is shown, never a shorter or zero
  one.
- Reward estimates come from the completed eras' `ErasValidatorReward`,
  annualized with the relay clock. Reward history that cannot be read gives a
  zero estimate, as for any validator whose return cannot be computed, rather
  than a failed screen.
- Validator identities come from the People chains.
- Both validator choice screens keep their choices disabled while the
  validator list loads. When it cannot be loaded (a timeout included) they
  show the error with Retry and enable the choices again; choosing one loads
  the list first, so a dismissed dialog or a rotated screen is never stuck.
- Redeem shows the redeemable amount as unavailable, with Retry, when the
  ledger cannot be read again (a Hub's active era included), and redeems only
  after a refresh succeeds.
- The staking balance screen shows Retry and keeps rebonding off when the
  unbonding list cannot be read (it needs the era clock); it does not close.
- The staking updaters classify the ledger with the active era that their own
  subscription caches, and classify it again whenever that era changes, so
  matured chunks become redeemable without another ledger change and a failed
  remote read cannot stop the updaters.
- No staking indexer answers with Hub rewards (the Polkadot and Kusama squids
  follow the relays, newest rewards 2025-11-04 and 2025-10-07), so reward
  history stays unsupported on the Hubs.

### Activation and older builds

The app reads `remote-config/production/chains/v13/chains.json` from master.
4.3.1 offers Hub staking only while that catalogue marks the Hub native asset
with `"staking": "assetHub"`. 4.3.0 maps any staking value it does not know to
unsupported and the Hubs carry no `poolStaking` option, so 4.3.0 keeps staking
off there: 4.3.0's own compiled mapping, run on the new catalogue, maps every
asset of all 50 networks exactly as on the old one. Builds 231 and 229 read the
shared-features-utils catalogue. Deleting the value turns 4.3.1's Hub staking
off. `scripts/verify-owned-remote-config.py` refuses relay-era staking values,
`poolStaking` and `crowdloans` on these networks, and the switch anywhere but a
Hub native asset.

### Upgrades

- A DOT or KSM staking or pool choice saved by any earlier build names the
  relay chain. It now opens the matching Hub when the wallet uses the same
  account on the relay and the Hub, which is the account whose stake moved
  there. A wallet with a different account on either one (an imported chain
  account) gets the chooser instead. The saved bytes are not rewritten.
  Without the catalogue switch the choice opens the chooser.
- The database schema stays at 84; `core-db/released-database-versions.txt`
  records `4.3.1+233=84`.

### Signing

- Every signed extension is checked against the runtime's portable metadata.
  An extension the app does not know is accepted only when the metadata proves
  it takes no bytes; a known one must reproduce exactly the bytes written. The
  signed and additional extras of all 34 reachable Substrate networks of the
  catalogue encode under this check (live metadata, 2026-10-30).
- Hub staking and pool calls require the complete signing context (genesis,
  versions, mortality, nonce, metadata mode, native fee).

### Also ported

- Validator search keeps its catalogue and selection bound to the network and
  account it was opened for, recovers after a failed load, and the shared
  computational cache no longer keeps a failed or cancelled value.
- A network-account JSON export reads the wallet once, and an Ethereum export
  carries the Ethereum seed rather than the Substrate one.

## Verification

Run locally, without hosted CI (owner's rule of 2026-10-25); logs, counts and
artifact hashes are kept with the source commit outside the source tree.

- Staking and pool calls: each is signed through the app's builder over the
  live Polkadot, Kusama and Westend Asset Hub metadata of 2026-10-30, and the
  payload compared with independent polkadot-js vectors. Kusama Asset Hub
  upgraded from 2003002 to 2005001 after the Codex fixtures were taken, so its
  metadata and vectors were refreshed.
- Payouts (withdrawn validators included), slash warnings, the per-account
  unbonding period, the reward model, the ledger's era classification and
  saved DOT/KSM choices (with chain accounts) have unit tests; the upgrade test
  runs against the production catalogue.
- Payout scan cost: the scan's requests, replayed against the live Hubs, are
  254 on Polkadot Asset Hub (50 258 pages, 194 unclaimed, 19.7 MB) and 275 on
  Kusama Asset Hub (58 077 pages, 2 883 unclaimed, 27.5 MB). With eight eras
  in flight they took 10 s and 24 s of the 180 s timeout, against 140 s and
  130 s one era at a time.
- Older builds: 4.3.0's compiled catalogue mapping maps every asset of the new
  catalogue as it maps the old one.
- Signing on every network: the extras of all 34 reachable Substrate networks
  of the catalogue encode under the portable-type check.
- The release gates: the unit-test modules with `--rerun`, `detektAll`,
  `:app:lintDebug`, `CI=true` strict release tests, the release script suites,
  `scripts/test-android-aab-identity.sh`, and the signed AAB verified with the
  production build script. Nothing is uploaded to Play.

## Not in this release

- Reward history on the Hubs (no indexer).
- Westend Asset Hub: the catalogue does not list it.
