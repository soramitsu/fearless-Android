## Why

On 8 November 2026 the owner asked for a better-looking app with the new logo: "the mobile apps are ugly and don't use
our new logo branding. make the ui look good and really cool with neumorphism and global illumination and subtle and
informative animations". The redesign stayed out of 4.3.2 so that the key-safety release could ship first.

### What users notice

- A new look and the winged-sun logo, also as the launcher icon.
- The wallet home scrolls as one page. Receive and Buy on the home pick an asset first.
- The launch goes from the splash to the wallet without a black screen, and the PIN pad follows the launch art
  directly: 4.3.1 and 4.3.2 show a dark "Recover your wallet PIN / Your stored PIN cannot be read" panel for a moment
  on every start, for as long as reading the PIN's state takes.
- New wallets establish EVM connections for their first balance scan; unreachable networks still remain unknown.
- Money shows its currency symbol again: `$0.09`, not `usd0.09`. 4.3.0 to 4.3.2 show the id.
- Settings > Manage assets and Start staking for a pool no longer crash, and all three pairs of tokens that share a
  currency id (tDOT and GLMR on Acala, RMRK and taiKSM on Karura, vKSM and vsKSM on Bifrost) get their own rows.
- On Android 13 and later, system Back closes sheets it only pushed down before (Send confirmation, pool and validator
  staking sheets, wallet score, error sheets and others). A confirmation that is being submitted stays open, and Back
  on a dApp request declines it like the close icon.
- TON signing and pairing can open from the wallet tabs. Google backup opens its password dialog after the required
  PIN check and returns its result to the originating wallet screen.
- The upgrade checks preserved wallet keys, the PIN and the existing WalletConnect session. The current catalogue lists fewer networks than 4.2.0.
- Most Production users are still on 4.2.0 (229) and would go straight to 4.3.3, so they also get everything in
  `docs/releases/4.3.2.md` under "What users notice" (one new Google Drive sign-in, biometric unlock may turn off
  once, a slower PIN check). The Play notes say so.

### Upgrade safety

This release includes presentation and navigation fixes plus a change to how a new wallet reads balances.
The owner approved the balance-read change with "Yes, do it here": the first scan opens each EVM network's
connection and waits up to 15 seconds for its client; the discovery sweep permits a Substrate network up to
45 seconds to load. The total still treats an unread balance as unknown. A new wallet can therefore continue
to show "Balance unavailable" while a counted network, such as Liberland, is unreachable.

The database remains schema 84. The PIN display fix changes the loading state before the PIN state is read;
PIN verification and recovery remain unchanged. The asset runtime identity used by stored descriptors is
unchanged; list row keys now distinguish individual chain assets. The final source diff and upgrade checks
are recorded alongside the exact source commit and artifact hashes. Earlier `ui-only-audit.txt` receipts
apply to their earlier candidate only and do not describe the final release.

### Android 16 network search

An Android 16 emulator with 16 KB pages recorded a 5.020-second input timeout on the first Shift key while entering
Karura. The main thread was returning from the Android renderer. HWUI frame records show outstanding renderer work
for 4.689 of the 5.020 seconds following the key event. Device CPU pressure was high. These timings measure queued
frames and do not establish how much of the delay each component caused.

The same installed release APK completed the original search route and displayed both Karura rows on two controlled
runs. On the original SwiftShader configuration, Android acknowledged the matched Shift key in 73.759 ms.
With Apple GPU acceleration it took 2.059 ms. The respective traces matched 58 of 58 and 26 of 26 dispatched keys
to their acknowledgements and reported zero parser error or data-loss counters. Both runs finished without a new
app crash or ANR. The SwiftShader run also verified the Bifrost duplicate rows and pool staking entry and Back.
The original incident, both traces and the assessment are bound by `api36-r8-smoke/requalification-receipt.json`
(SHA-256 `0cd7e35b3c0194aea3134ca5fd9b3e3ad8644de0d4d388904c66935f46a98d9d`). Host load also varied between runs.
The incident's frequency on production phones remains unknown.

The release-derived smoke APK was signed for local testing and has SHA-256
`7dd084b031f13c0573038c9919a659b75a047b2a21d9c9a43ab6bd71ae9ff752`. The 229 upgrade used a matching disposable signer.
The checked scope covers emulator installations. Physical Play installs, live TON bridge and Google Drive account
OAuth remain unverified.
