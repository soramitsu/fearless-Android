## Candidate identity

- Package: `jp.co.soramitsu.fearless`
- Version name: `4.3.3`
- Version code: `235` (4.3.2 is 234)
- Room database: schema `84`, unchanged since 4.3.0 (`core-db/released-database-versions.txt`: `4.3.3+235=84`)
- Upload certificate: SHA-256
  `AD:6C:1D:07:BE:5D:FA:8C:53:9E:8C:9B:8B:D4:3D:C1:C6:CF:79:A8:39:82:8B:10:B6:F9:53:AD:57:C1:BD:E8`,
  as for 4.3.2.
- Branch: `release/sunlit-redesign`, targeting `master` in PR #1276, after PR #1275.
- Final bundle: pending, recorded below once built.
- Play: not uploaded. When 234 was saved and sent for review on 9 November 2026, 233's running staged rollout went to
  halted, most likely because of that save, though Play's activity log and Google's help don't state a cause. Until the
  new release is published no device receives either version, so the upload waits until 4.3.2's rollout is settled.

## Why

On 8 November 2026 the owner asked for a better-looking app with the new logo: "the mobile apps are ugly and don't use
our new logo branding. make the ui look good and really cool with neumorphism and global illumination and subtle and
informative animations". The redesign stayed out of 4.3.2 so that the key-safety release could ship first.

### What users notice

- A new look and the winged-sun logo, also as the launcher icon.
- The wallet home scrolls as one page. Receive and Buy on the home pick an asset first.
- The launch goes from the splash to the wallet without a black screen.
- Money shows its currency symbol again: `$0.09`, not `usd0.09`. 4.3.0 to 4.3.2 show the id.
- Settings > Manage assets and Start staking for a pool no longer crash, and all three pairs of tokens that share a
  currency id (tDOT and GLMR on Acala, RMRK and taiKSM on Karura, vKSM and vsKSM on Bifrost) get their own rows.
- On Android 13 and later, system Back closes sheets it only pushed down before (Send confirmation, pool and validator
  staking sheets, wallet score, error sheets and others). A confirmation that is being submitted stays open, and Back
  on a dApp request declines it like the close icon.
- Keys, addresses, PIN and connected dApps stay as they are.
- Most Production users are still on 4.2.0 (229) and would go straight to 4.3.3, so they also get everything in
  `docs/releases/4.3.2.md` under "What users notice" (one new Google Drive sign-in, biometric unlock may turn off
  once, a slower PIN check). The Play notes say so.

### Upgrade safety

Presentation only, by spec. The diff from `a168420d9` touches no key, signing, storage, networking, remote configuration
or chain code; the receipt is `ui-only-audit.txt` in the evidence below. Outside screens and resources it changes when
night mode is set (`App.onCreate`), how the avatar in `runtime-utils/.../icon` is drawn (with its source pins), and which
currency symbol money is shown with (read from the bundled currency list; the selected id stays the currency's identity).
The runtime identity of an asset is unchanged, because stored metadata descriptors use it; only list row keys moved.
