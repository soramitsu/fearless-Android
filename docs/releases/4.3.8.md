# Fearless Android 4.3.8 (240)

A crash-fix release on the exact 4.3.7 (239) source,
`ca0928f0f49c631629eba21bc2e4ca2a1d3edf61`. Nothing else from the
consolidated line is included. The release ledger records `4.3.8+240=84`:
database schema 84 and every supported migration are unchanged.

## What Play reported for 4.3.7

Play Console, last seven days on 24 November 2026: eight user-perceived
crashes on 239, in two clusters. Play showed no ANR cluster for 239.

| Cluster | Events | Users | Devices |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `IconGenerator.generateSquaresRotation` `ArrayIndexOutOfBoundsException` | 7 | 2 | Galaxy A54 5G, Galaxy S26 Ultra (Android 17) |
| `ChainsRepository$getChain$2` `IllegalStateException` | 1 | 1 | HONOR X6a (Android 14) |

## Fixes

**EVM icon.** Since 4.3.0 the EVM wallet icon has been drawn from the
19-byte seed `fearless-wallet-evm`, and the square layout read byte 31 of its
input. Every call threw. Two screens made that call: Send, as soon as a valid
recipient was entered on any EVM network, and the wallet home of a wallet
holding only an Ethereum account, on every open. WalletConnect's 20-byte EVM
account ids hit the same code. `IconGenerator.generateEthereumAddressIcon` now
reads an input shorter than 32 bytes as if it were zero-padded. Inputs of 32
bytes or more draw exactly as before. The R8 mapping of 239 places the Play
frame in `StatelessAddressIconGenerator$createWalletIcon$2`.

**TON Connect bridge.** The root screen subscribes to the QR bridge at start.
For a wallet with a QR connection, the subscription looked TON up with the
strict 60-second `getChain`, and a missing TON entry (or one without a bridge
address) ended the process. The bridge address now comes from the live
catalog. With no TON or no usable bridge address the bridge stays idle, and it
subscribes when TON appears. Connections and the address are combined per
wallet, so a wallet switch never subscribes the previous wallet and an
unrelated catalog update does not reopen the stream. A failure anywhere in the
bridge, including a stored session key that cannot be read, stops only the
bridge.

**NFT pages.** The wallet home subscribes to NFT collections even on the
Currencies tab. Each page looked its chain up with the strict `getChain`. A
chain that left the catalog after its page was requested made the lookup
time out and crashed the home screen. Each batch of pages now reads one
catalog snapshot without waiting, and a page for a missing chain is dropped.

**TON-only home.** The loading placeholder of a TON-only wallet took the first
catalog asset and threw on an empty catalog. It now shows no placeholder.

The Play sample does not name the `getChain` caller. These are the two
automatic callers that a static audit of 239 found. Lookups that only follow a
tap (Retry, Hide/Show, Buy, the TON QR approval sheet) and the title of a
restored Switch node screen are unchanged.

## Not changed

A saved network filter that names a retired network makes the home wait up to
60 seconds before showing All networks. The home stays usable while it waits.
This is left for a reviewed change (see `docs/roadmap.md`).
