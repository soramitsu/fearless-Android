## Google Play

Google Play confirmed internal availability of build 237 and accepted the Production change for review on 13 November 2026. The release uses the project’s documented initial stage of 10% in all eligible countries. At submission Play estimated 803 of 8,026 active installs. The Production preview showed a warning about missing native debug symbols. The Internal preview additionally flagged the new download size of 28.3 MB, up 7.4 MB. Device coverage was unchanged. Managed publishing was enabled.

On 13 November 2026 at 12:49 UTC, Google Play published 237. The Production track reported “Google Play でユーザーの 10% に公開” in all 178 countries and regions. Build 236 was marked superseded. Build 229 remained available outside the new cohort.
